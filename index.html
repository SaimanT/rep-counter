A free online rep counter for your workouts

It's easy to lose count in your head — especially on rep 30 or your third set. This counter keeps track for you: set a goal, then press space after every rep (or tap your phone screen). The huge numbers are readable from across the room, and right below them you see how many you've done and how many are left.

How to use it

  • Set a goal — type a number or pick one of the presets.
  • Press space after every rep. On a phone, tap the screen.
  • Miscounted? Backspace removes one rep.
  • Finished a set? Press Enter. The set is saved, the count starts over, and you see the total below — for example “Sets: 20 · 20 · 18 — workout total: 58”.
  • Goal reached — the screen turns green and a sound plays.
  • F toggles full screen, R resets the whole workout.
  • On a phone the same actions are buttons under the counter: −1, Set, Reset, sound and vibration, full screen. The screen stays on while the page is open.

Works for any exercise

Use it for anything you count: push-ups, squats, pull-ups, lunges, sit-ups, burpees, kettlebell swings. It's handy for challenges like “100 push-ups a day”: set a goal of 100, split it into sets, and the counter adds them up. Popular exercises have their own counters with ready-made goals and tips:

Install it as an app

You can install the counter on your computer or phone — it opens in its own window and works offline. In Chrome or Edge, click “Install app” at the top of the page or the install icon in the address bar. On iPhone: Share → Add to Home Screen.

FAQ

Is it free?

Yes, completely free — no sign-up and no ads.

Does the counter work offline?

Yes. After the first visit the page works without internet, and you can install it as an app on your computer or phone.

Will my count be saved if I close the tab?

Yes. Your count, goal and sets are saved in your browser on this device. Nothing is sent anywhere.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Every tap on the screen adds one rep.
