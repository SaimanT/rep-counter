How to count lunges

Press space or tap the screen as you return to standing. For alternating lunges, count every step: a goal of 20 means 10 per leg. If you do one leg first and then the other, save them as separate sets with Enter.

Controls: Space +1, Backspace −1, Enter finishes a set, R resets. On a phone, tap the screen and use the buttons under the counter. More about the counter.

How many lunges should you do?

  • Beginner: 3 sets of 8–10 per leg.
  • Intermediate: 3–4 sets of 12–15 per leg, optionally with dumbbells.

Form in brief

  • Take a step long enough for both knees to bend to about 90°.
  • Front knee stays over the foot — not far past the toes and not caving in.
  • Keep your torso upright and lower straight down.

FAQ

Do I count lunges per leg or both together?

Whichever you prefer — just count the same way every workout. Most people count each leg separately: save the left and right legs as separate sets with Enter.

How many lunges should a beginner do?

Start with 3 sets of 8–10 reps per leg and add 2 reps a week.
