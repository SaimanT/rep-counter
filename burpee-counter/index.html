How to count burpees

Burpees are fast, and almost everyone loses count halfway through a set. Put the screen in front of you and press space or tap your phone on the floor at the end of every jump — the counter shows how many are left until your goal.

Controls: Space +1, Backspace −1, Enter finishes a set, R resets. On a phone, tap the screen and use the buttons under the counter. More about the counter.

How many burpees should you do?

  • Beginner: 3 sets of 5–10 reps, without the push-up and jump if needed.
  • Intermediate: 3–5 sets of 10–20.
  • “100 burpees” challenge: goal of 100, split into sets and save each with Enter.

Form in brief

  • Squat → plank → push-up → back to squat → jump up with a clap overhead.
  • Keep your body straight in the plank, don't sag.
  • Land softly on slightly bent knees.

FAQ

How many burpees should I do?

Start with 3 sets of 10 and add 1–2 reps per set each week. Intense workouts go up to 50–100 burpees per session.

How do I stop losing count of burpees?

Press space or tap the screen right after each jump — the counter keeps track for you, and the “left” line shows how many remain.

Is it free, and does it work offline?

Yes: no sign-up, no ads, and after the first visit the page works without internet. Your count is saved in the browser.
