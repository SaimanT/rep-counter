How to count push-ups

Place your laptop or phone where you can see it from the plank position. Press space after every push-up — or put your phone on the floor in front of you and tap the screen. The big numbers are visible out of the corner of your eye, and the “left” line helps you finish the set.

Controls: Space +1, Backspace −1, Enter finishes a set, R resets. On a phone, tap the screen and use the buttons under the counter. More about the counter.

How many push-ups should you do?

  • Beginner: 3 sets of 5–10 reps, on your knees or against a bench if needed.
  • Intermediate: 3–4 sets of 15–25 reps.
  • “100 push-ups a day” challenge: set a goal of 100 and do it in sets throughout the day — press Enter after each one and the counter shows your daily total.

Add 1–2 reps per set every few workouts for steady progress.

Form in brief

  • Keep a straight line from head to heels — don't let your hips sag.
  • Elbows at about 45° to your body, not flared out.
  • Lower until your chest nearly touches the floor, then fully straighten your arms.
  • Count only clean reps — that's what the counter is for.

FAQ

How many push-ups should I do a day?

It depends on your level. Beginners do well with 20–30 push-ups spread over a few sets, trained people with 50–100. Consistency and clean form matter more than a record number.

Can I count push-ups with my phone?

Yes. Put your phone on the floor in front of you and tap the screen at the top of each rep — every tap counts one push-up.

Is it free, and does it work offline?

Yes: no sign-up, no ads, and after the first visit the page works without internet. Your count is saved in the browser.
