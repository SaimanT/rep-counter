How to count ab exercises

Put your phone next to you on the mat and tap the screen after every crunch — or keep a keyboard within reach and press space. The numbers are big enough to read lying down. Save each exercise as a separate set with Enter.

Controls: Space +1, Backspace −1, Enter finishes a set, R resets. On a phone, tap the screen and use the buttons under the counter. More about the counter.

How many reps?

  • Crunches or sit-ups: 3 sets of 15–30 reps.
  • Lying leg raises: 3 sets of 10–20.
  • Bicycle crunches: 3 sets of 20–40 touches (each side is one rep).

Form in brief

  • Keep your lower back on the floor and don't pull on your head.
  • Lift with your abs, slowly and without jerking.
  • Breathe out on the effort, in on the way down.

FAQ

How many sit-ups should I do a day?

3–4 sets of 15–30 reps, 3–4 times a week, is usually enough. Your abs need rest between workouts too.

Do I count each side of a bicycle crunch?

Usually each elbow-to-knee touch counts as one rep. What matters is counting the same way every workout so you can see progress.

Is it free, and does it work offline?

Yes: no sign-up, no ads, and after the first visit the page works without internet. Your count is saved in the browser.
