How to count squats

Stand facing the screen. A wireless keyboard or a remote with a spacebar in your hand works best — or put your phone nearby and tap it at the top of every squat. The numbers are readable from a couple of metres away, so you never have to bend down to check.

Controls: Space +1, Backspace −1, Enter finishes a set, R resets. On a phone, tap the screen and use the buttons under the counter. More about the counter.

How many squats should you do?

  • Beginner: 3 sets of 10–15 reps.
  • Intermediate: 3–4 sets of 20–30 reps.
  • “100 squats a day” challenge: goal of 100, sets of 20–25 with rest in between — the counter adds them up for you.

Form in brief

  • Feet shoulder-width apart, toes turned slightly out.
  • Knees track over your toes and don't cave in.
  • Keep your back straight and your heels on the floor.
  • Go at least to parallel if your mobility allows.

FAQ

How many squats should I do a day?

Start with 30–50 squats over a few sets. Trained people do 100 or more. Increase the load gradually and watch your knees.

How do I count squats when my hands hold dumbbells?

Do the set, then add the reps with the spacebar and save the set with Enter. Or ask a partner to press for you.

Is it free, and does it work offline?

Yes: no sign-up, no ads, and after the first visit the page works without internet. Your count is saved in the browser.
