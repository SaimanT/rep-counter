One burpee at a time

Burpees are where the head gives up before the body: “fifteen?!” Don’t think about fifteen. Do one, stand up, tap the phone on the floor. Breathe. Now just one more. The counter keeps the score, and “Just one” mode hides the goal if it feels too far.

Controls: Space +1, Backspace −1, Enter finishes a set, R resets, the 1× button hides the goal. On a phone, tap the screen. About the one-rep method.

If even one burpee is hard

  1. Step-back burpee — step back into the plank and step in again, no jump.
  2. Incline burpee — hands on a bench or a low table.
  3. No push-up — plank only, then back up.
  4. Full burpee — with the push-up and the jump.

How many burpees should you do?

Start with sets of 5 at an easy version and add a rep or two each week. For a 100-burpee challenge, split it into small sets and save each one with Enter.

Form in brief

  • Squat → plank → (push-up) → back to squat → jump.
  • Keep your body straight in the plank.
  • Land softly on slightly bent knees.

FAQ

I’m out of breath after two burpees. Is that normal?

Yes — burpees are hard on everyone. Use the step-back version and rest between reps; one at a time still counts.

How many burpees should I do?

Start with 3 sets of 5–10 and add 1–2 reps per set each week. Intense workouts go up to 50–100.
